Continuous Gravitational Waves
2026 Workshop

A focused meeting on continuous gravitational wave science, neutron stars, data analysis, and future prospects, as a satellite meeting to the 2026 LVK-meeting.

September 14-18, 2026 Invitation Deadline: July 24, 2026 IUCAA, Pune, India
Invitation requests are closed

About the workshop

The LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA (LVK) collaboration meeting shall take place in IUCAA, India during 21-24 Sept 2026. It is being organized by the Inter-University Centre for Astronomy and Astrophysics (IUCAA), Pune. As a satellite meeting of the LVK meeting, a meeting focussed on Continuous Gravitational waves will be held in IUCAA from 14-18 Sept.

This satellite workshop will bring together leading experts on CWs, neutron stars and dark matter, both inside and outside the LVK, to discuss timely results and to build sustainable collaborations. It is the 3rd edition in a series of meetings designed to promote discussions on CW science.

The meeting will be small and interactive, and participation will be on an invitation-only basis. If you want to participate but have not received an invitation, please register below, indicating your academic affiliation and your connection to the field. If you can't make it in person, you can also indicate whether you would like to follow the proceedings online.

Scientific scope

Topics covered

The workshop spans the breadth of continuous gravitational wave science. Below are the thematic areas we expect to cover.

  • Theory - Neutron stars, Dark matter, Astrophysical Interpretation, Astrophysics informed observation strategies
  • Search methodologies, DetChar, Machine Learning Methods for CW Searches
  • Current search results ((LVK and beyond): Isolated All-sky searches, Narrowband and targeted searches, Searches for NS in Binary Systems, Directed Searches for isolated NS)
  • Synergies between GW and EM
  • LIGO India, ET, CE current status and plans, impact on CW searches
  • Discussion on the current state and future prospects of CW research

Travel

Travel and Visa information

IUCAA is located at the north end of the campus of the University of Pune. The city of Pune is about 170 km south-east of Mumbai (Bombay) along the Mumbai-Pune expressway. Pune lies at an altitude of 559 m and the climate is mostly pleasant.

Visitors arriving at Pune by air can take a taxi from the Lohegaon Airport, Pune, which is about 13 km from IUCAA. Visitors arriving at Pune by train can take a taxi for the University of Pune and follow the signs to IUCAA from the University Main Gate.

Visitors arriving at Pune via Mumbai can either take a train from Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus to Pune Railway Station or a bus from Dadar bus stand, from where air-conditioned buses leave frequently for Pune. These take about three and a half hours to reach Pune.

Visitors can also reserve an airport transfer using ECRS.
